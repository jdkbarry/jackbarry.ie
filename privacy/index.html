Privacy notice

How we look after your personal data

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Who we are

Jack Barry Solicitor, 89 Farmhill Manor, Sligo, County Sligo, F91 CC8H, is a law firm providing legal services, mainly in property. Jack Barry, Solicitor, runs the firm and is the Data Controller for the personal data described in this notice.

Questions about this notice or your data can go to Jack Barry at info@jackbarry.ie or 071 9333 040. As a small firm we have a data protection contact point rather than a Data Protection Officer, and that contact point is Jack.

What we collect and why

Personal data is any information that identifies you. We collect it from you directly (for example by email, phone, post, or the quote page) and from others involved in your matter, such as lenders, estate agents and the other side’s solicitor.

What we collectWhy we use itOur legal basis
Your name, phone number, email address and details of your property or matter, when you ask for a quote or contact us.To reply to you, prepare a quote and answer your questions before you decide to instruct us.Steps taken at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
Identification, proof of address, PPS number, details of your finances, your lender, the property and the other side, once you instruct us.To act for you in your purchase, sale, Mortgage or other property matter, and to register it at the Land Registry or Registry of Deeds and pay stamp duty.Contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)), including anti-money-laundering checks.

Anything you tell us in an email, letter or call.To deal with your enquiry or complaint and to keep a record of it.Contract, legal obligation or legitimate interests, depending on the matter.

Giving us this information is needed for us to act for you. If you do not give it, we cannot do the work or complete the legal checks we are required to carry out.

This website

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes, under its own privacy statement.
  • The estimate on the quote page is worked out in your browser. Nothing you type there is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The Send my request button opens an email in your own email app, with your details filled in. Nothing reaches us until you press Send in that email. The email then comes to us in the ordinary way, as described under “Who we share it with”.
  • Please do not send identity documents or bank details through the quote page. We will ask for those separately once you instruct us.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only where we need to, and only what is needed. The main recipients are:

  • Your lender, the other side’s solicitor, the estate agent and the management company, where relevant to your matter.
  • Tailte Éireann (which runs the Land Registry), the Revenue Commissioners and other public bodies we must deal with on your behalf.
  • Barristers, engineers, search providers and other professionals we instruct for your matter.
  • Our service providers, including the provider of our email and file storage (Microsoft 365), IT support and our professional indemnity insurers and their brokers, if a claim or a notification arises.
  • The Law Society of Ireland, the Legal Services Regulatory Authority or a court, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.

Transfers outside the EEA

Some of our providers, including Microsoft and GitHub, may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep conveyancing files for 12 years after the matter ends, and then destroy them securely. We keep details from an enquiry that does not lead to instructions only for as long as we need them to deal with that enquiry. Where the law requires us to keep records for longer, we do.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • ask us to erase your data in certain circumstances;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, our processing;
  • ask for your data in a form you can move to someone else; and
  • not be subject to a decision made only by automated means.

These rights are not absolute, and some of the data in your file is covered by legal professional privilege or by our legal duties, which can limit what we can disclose or delete. To use any of them, email info@jackbarry.ie or call 071 9333 040. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at www.dataprotection.ie.
