Selling a home

Most delays in a sale come from the paperwork. Getting it together before you find a buyer saves weeks later.

Get a quote for this

What to have ready

  • Where your Title Deeds are held, usually with your lender if you have a Mortgage
  • Planning documents for any extension or alteration
  • BER certificate and Local Property Tax details
  • Management company details, for an apartment or managed estate
  • Septic tank registration, if the property has one
