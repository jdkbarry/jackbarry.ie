Self-Build

Building your own home

A Self-Build has two legal parts: getting the site into your name, and putting your lender's Mortgage in place so the loan can be released in stages as you build.

Get a quote for this

What to have ready

  • The folio and map for the site, or details of who owns it now
  • Your planning permission
  • Your loan offer and its stage payment conditions
  • Your architect's or engineer's details
  • Photo ID, proof of address and your PPS number

See your costs in under a minute

Press Get a quote for this, answer a few questions and your full costs appear straight away. Jack then confirms them in writing before you decide to go ahead.

How it works, step by step

  1. 1

    Getting started

    You get your costs in writing, we check your ID and review the site and your planning permission.

  2. 2

    Signing Legal Documents

    The site is transferred to you if needed and you sign the Mortgage.

  3. 3

    Drawdown

    The first stage payment is released and the build begins.

  4. 4

    After closing

    We register you as owner of the site and register the Mortgage.
