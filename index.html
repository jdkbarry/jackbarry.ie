Working with Jack

You do not need to come to an office. Jack can come to you.

  • Phone, email and post

    Most of the work is done by phone, email and post, so you do not need to take time off to sit in a waiting room.

  • Signing your documents

    Where a document can be signed electronically, it is. Where it has to be signed in front of a solicitor, Jack arranges a time and place that suits you.

    If you would rather sit down and talk it through, Jack can meet you in Sligo or travel to you, wherever suits.

About Jack

Jack Barry has specialised in buying and selling property since he qualified as a solicitor in 2019.

Alongside everyday sales and purchases, he sorts out the problems that stall a sale, such as land that was never registered, lost Deeds and Land Registry maps that do not match the ground.

He runs his own practice in Sligo and handles every case himself. The person you speak to on day one is the person who closes your sale.
