Switching your Mortgage

Your new lender needs a solicitor to check the property's ownership and put its Mortgage in place. We deal with both lenders for you.

Get a quote for this

What to have ready

  • Your new loan offer
  • Your existing Mortgage account number and lender
  • Photo ID, proof of address and your PPS number
  • Details of any changes to the property since you bought it

See your costs in under a minute

Press Get a quote for this, answer a few questions and your full costs appear straight away. Jack then confirms them in writing before you decide to go ahead.

How it works, step by step

  1. 1

    Getting started

    You get your costs in writing, we check your ID and collect your Deeds from your current lender.

  2. 2

    Reviewing Title

    We check the property for the new lender and you sign the new Mortgage.

  3. 3

    Drawdown

    The new loan is paid out and your old Mortgage is cleared.

  4. 4

    After closing

    We register the new Mortgage and remove the old one.
