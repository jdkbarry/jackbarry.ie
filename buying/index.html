Buying a home

From sale agreed to keys in hand, including the checks on the property, its planning and your loan that protect you before you sign.

What to have ready

  • Your loan approval, or proof of funds if you are buying without a Mortgage
  • The estate agent's sale details, once the sale is agreed
  • Photo ID, proof of address and your PPS number
  • Where your deposit and the rest of the money are coming from
  • Help to Buy or First Home Scheme details, if you are using them

How it works, step by step

  1. 1

    Getting started

    You get your costs in writing, we check your ID and ask the seller's solicitor for the contracts.

  2. 2

    Contracts

    We check the property's ownership and planning history, raise questions, explain the contract to you and you sign.

  3. 3

    Closing

    Your lender releases the loan, the balance is paid and you get the keys.

  4. 4

    After closing

    We pay your stamp duty and register you as the owner.
